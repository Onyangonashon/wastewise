🌱 SMARTER CHOICES

Reduce. Reuse. Recycle.

Small changes in our daily habits can help reduce waste.

⬇️

Reduce

Use fewer unnecessary products and avoid excessive packaging.

  • Buy only what you need
  • Avoid unnecessary packaging
  • Choose reusable items
🔄

Reuse

Use items again instead of throwing them away after one use.

  • Reuse containers
  • Repair useful items
  • Donate things you no longer need
♻️

Recycle

Sort suitable materials so they can be processed into new products.

  • Sort recyclable materials
  • Keep recyclables clean
  • Follow local recycling rules
