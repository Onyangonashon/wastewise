Collection Day Waste Category Examples Preparation
Monday 📄 Paper & Plastic Paper, cardboard, plastic bottles Keep items clean and dry.

COLLECT RESPONSIBLY

Prepare Your Waste

Follow these simple steps before collection day.

🧹

1. Sort Your Waste

Separate paper, plastic, glass, metal, organic waste, and general rubbish into appropriate containers.

🧴

2. Clean Recyclables

Empty bottles and containers, and follow local recycling instructions before placing them out.

🗑️

3. Use Suitable Bins

Store waste in suitable containers with lids to discourage pests and prevent litter.

⏰

4. Check Collection Times

Follow your local collection service's instructions about when and where to put waste out.
