♻️ SORTING GUIDE

Sort Waste the Right Way

Follow these simple steps to make waste sorting easier.

1

Separate

Separate recyclable, organic and other waste into appropriate groups.

2

Clean

Empty and clean suitable recyclable containers before placing them aside.

3

Keep Dry

Keep paper and cardboard dry so they remain suitable for recycling.

4

Check Local Rules

Recycling rules can differ between communities, so check local guidelines.

5

Dispose Safely

Take electronic and hazardous waste to suitable collection facilities.

⚠️ Important: Always follow your local waste collection guidelines. Do not place hazardous materials in ordinary recycling containers.
