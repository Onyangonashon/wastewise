🗑️ WASTE TYPES

Know Your Waste

Understanding different types of waste makes sorting easier and more effective.

🧴

Plastic

Bottles, containers, packaging and other plastic products.

♻️ Check if it is recyclable
📄

Paper

Newspapers, office paper, cardboard and paper packaging.

📦 Keep paper clean and dry
🍾

Glass

Glass bottles, jars and other suitable glass containers.

⚠️ Handle broken glass carefully
🍌

Organic Waste

Food scraps, leaves and other biodegradable materials.

🌱 Consider composting
💻

Electronic Waste

Old phones, computers, cables and electronic equipment.

🔋 Use special collection points
⚠️

Hazardous Waste

Materials that require special handling and disposal.

🛑 Never mix with normal waste
