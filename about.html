WHO WE ARE

Welcome to WasteWise

WasteWise is an educational project designed to help people understand waste management and make environmentally responsible choices.

We provide simple information about different types of waste, sorting, collection, reuse and recycling.

To encourage people to reduce waste, reuse useful materials and dispose of waste responsibly.
