WHO WE ARE

Welcome to WasteWise

WasteWise is an educational project created to help people understand waste management and make environmentally responsible choices.

Our goal is to make waste management easier to understand by providing simple information about waste types, sorting, collection, reuse and recycling.

Small actions can make a difference. By learning how to handle waste correctly, individuals and communities can contribute to cleaner surroundings.
